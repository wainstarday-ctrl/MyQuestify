MyQuestify is a task tracker built like a game. The hours you estimate for a task turn into in-app currency, and that currency unlocks interactive scenes you can play with while thinking about your next step.

The app runs entirely on your device. It does not ask for network access: there is nothing to reach out to. A build will fail if network access ever appears in it — the permission set is checked against an approved list on every release.

WHAT IS INSIDE

• Quests with priorities and deadlines. Priority sets the reward multiplier. Missing a deadline costs half the reward, but you can still finish late: forbidding it would punish exactly the behaviour the app should encourage.

• Thought chains — breaking a task into a tree of steps of any depth. Two views: a nested outline and a web graph whose nodes you can drag around.

• Ten interactive scenes on a custom rendering engine: a garden with falling fruit, a volcano with flowing lava, meshed gears, a solar system, a pond with waves, a desk with scattered objects, an elastic net, a campfire with a burn model, storm clouds, and a chemistry lab with eight reactions. Scenes unlock with earned currency; locked ones can be previewed for free.

• A deadline calendar and monthly summaries.

• Deadline reminders arrive while the app is closed.

• Two interface languages, light and dark themes.

ABOUT THE LANGUAGE MODEL

The app includes an Oracle — a companion that helps you break a task down. This build ships without a language model, so it replies with prepared phrases. A separate release carrying a 0.5B model that runs on the device is available on the project releases page; it needs 4 GB of RAM or more.

DATA

Everything stays on the device and is never sent anywhere. No analytics, no Google services: their absence is verified at build time.
